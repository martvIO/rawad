## [2026-06-02 03:40] session | Fix "صورك" camera restart loop
Touched: Digital Invitations / Face page (DigitalYourPhotos.jsx). Reported: tapping into the photos camera showed the live video for ~1s, then "جاري تحميل مكتبة التعرف" again, repeating. Root cause: subscribeInviteToken uses createPoller, which re-fires its callback on EVERY poll (poller.js explicitly does not dedupe), and Step-1's callback unconditionally called setStage("loading-models") each time → the whole camera flow restarted every ~3s. (Loop pre-existed but was masked by the earlier black-screen bug; once the camera actually rendered it became visible.) Fix: a routedRef guard routes the stage ONCE — after entering the camera flow, later polls only refresh groomUid and never reset the stage; the not-published branch stays unlocked so it still auto-advances when the groom publishes. Also reset cancelledRef on mount (remount safety) and stop any prior MediaStream before re-acquiring (clean retry, no double camera stream). Verified live on dawa.to (fake camera): camera view stays stable 12s with loadingReappeared=0, no console/page errors (the subsequent "انتهت مهلة الفحص" is the normal 12s liveness timeout because the fake camera has no real face). Build passes; deployed hosting; committed ebf6982.
