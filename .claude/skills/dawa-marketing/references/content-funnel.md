# Content Calendar & Funnel for Dawa

Produce the plan that makes the ads and videos add up: an organic posting calendar and the funnel that carries a stranger from first view to a paid package — through WhatsApp, where Dawa closes.

## The funnel (design backward from the WhatsApp close)

| Stage | Goal | What runs here | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TOFU — Awareness** | Get discovered by couples planning a wedding | Broad Reels/TikTok (education, emotional, reveal), broad cold ads | Reach, 3-sec views, CTR |
| **MOFU — Consideration** | Earn trust, prove it works | Retargeting ads, testimonials, demo videos, DMs, comments | Profile visits, video ≥50%, WhatsApp opens |
| **BOFU — Conversion** | Close on WhatsApp | Click-to-WhatsApp ads, package offer, seasonal/limited promo | Cost per conversation, bookings |
| **Post — Delight/Referral** | Turn customers into proof | Ask for testimonial + wedding footage → feeds TOFU | Reviews, referrals, UGC |

Cold traffic rarely buys on the first touch — a big wedding is a considered purchase with family involved. Retargeting (MOFU) is where most conversions actually come from, so **always run a retargeting ad set** alongside cold.

## Organic calendar (weekly cadence)

Aim for **4–5 posts/week**, rotating the [`video-content.md`](video-content.md) pillars so you're not only selling:

| Day | Pillar | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Education | "٣ أخطاء بتوزيع دعوات العرس" |
| Tue | Product demo | Digital-invite reveal screen-record |
| Wed | Social proof | Real couple testimonial / RSVP screen |
| Thu | Emotional / proof-of-delivery | Driver delivering, guest reaction |
| Fri–Sat | Seasonal / engagement | Weekend is prime wedding-content time in-market |

Reuse each concept across Reels + TikTok + Facebook. Batch-shoot a week in one session. Pin a WhatsApp-link comment on every post.

## Seasonality

Wedding season in the market peaks **spring–summer**, with engagements clustering around holidays. Ramp spend and content **6–8 weeks before** the season (couples book vendors early). Off-season: shift to awareness + email/WhatsApp list-building so you're top-of-mind when planning starts. Confirm the specific dates being targeted from the run inputs.

## WhatsApp nurture flow (BOFU — the close)

The ad's job is to start this chat; this flow closes it. Draft it as a sequence the user can run (or template into WhatsApp Business quick-replies):

1. **Greet + qualify:** "أهلين! مبروك 🎉 امتى العرس، وكم عدد المدعوين تقريباً، ومن أي منطقة؟"
2. **Diagnose:** map guest count + region + date to the right package (physical delivery reach, digital invites, RSVP tracking).
3. **Show, don't tell:** send a **live digital-invitation demo link** and a proof-of-delivery photo — the product sells itself.
4. **Recommend + price:** propose Premium or VIP with the current price and what's included; anchor on the stress it removes.
5. **Handle the family factor:** offer to send a summary they can forward to whoever decides.
6. **Close:** confirm date, take the guest list, onboard. Set a follow-up if they go quiet ("لسا بتفكروا؟ دورنا بيمتلي بسرعة بموسم الأعراس").

## Lead magnets (fuel MOFU→BOFU)

- **Free digital-invitation demo** — a personalized sample sent to their WhatsApp. Highest-intent, uses the actual product.
- **Free guest-list / RSVP template** in exchange for a message.
- **Seasonal promo** — early-bird package discount before wedding season.

## Deliver

When asked for a plan, output: **(1)** the funnel mapped to Dawa's specific content/ads per stage, **(2)** a concrete 1–4 week calendar with post concepts, **(3)** the WhatsApp nurture script, and **(4)** which lead magnet to run. Tie every stage to a real asset (a video from [`video-content.md`](video-content.md), an ad from [`meta-ads.md`](meta-ads.md)) so the plan is executable, not abstract.
