# Dawa Marketing

Grow دعوة (Dawa) by getting engaged couples in the Arab/Israeli market to start a WhatsApp conversation and buy a package. This skill turns "help me market" into a concrete deliverable — an ad campaign, a video script, ad copy, or a content plan — grounded in what Dawa actually is.

## First: confirm the run inputs

Marketing is specific or it's noise. Before producing anything, get these from the user (ask only for the ones the task needs — don't interrogate):

- **Goal** — what this campaign/post/video is for: bookings, WhatsApp leads, awareness, promoting a specific package.
- **Offer** — current price of each package (Premium / VIP), any promo or discount, and what's included right now.
- **Audience slice** — whole market, or a city/region (e.g. الناصرة, حيفا, المثلث, النقب), a language (Arabic / Hebrew), or a segment (couples vs. wedding planners).
- **Budget & dates** — daily ad budget and the wedding date / season being targeted.
- **Assets & handles** — Instagram/Facebook handle, landing URL, WhatsApp number, and any real photos/videos/testimonials available.

If the user doesn't know a value, mark it as a placeholder in the deliverable (e.g. `[PRICE]`, `[WHATSAPP]`) rather than inventing it.

## Core brand context — respect on every task

Every deliverable, in every branch, must hold to this. Don't restate it back to the user; apply it.

- **Who we sell to:** engaged couples and their families planning a wedding in Arab communities in Israel. Arab weddings are large (hundreds to thousands of guests), invitations are traditionally hand-delivered, and the whole family is involved in the decision. Secondary buyer: wedding planners.
- **What we sell:** end-to-end wedding-invitation management — a guest list with real-time RSVP tracking, **drivers who physically deliver printed invitations and upload proof photos**, and **personalized digital invitations sent per-guest over WhatsApp** (animated designs, envelope reveal, live starfield backgrounds).
- **The core pains we relieve:** the chaos of a huge guest list, not knowing who's actually coming (RSVP accuracy), and the burden of delivering invitations by hand across a region — solved with proof-of-delivery and instant WhatsApp digital invites.
- **Where the market lives:** Facebook + Instagram + TikTok for discovery; **WhatsApp is where deals actually happen** — Dawa is WhatsApp-native, so marketing should drive to a WhatsApp conversation, not a cold form, whenever possible.
- **Voice:** warm, celebratory, family-respecting, and modern. Write social/ad copy in **colloquial Palestinian/Israeli-Arabic** (not formal MSA) so it reads native; provide a **Hebrew** variant when the Hebrew-speaking segment is in scope. Never imply you personally know the reader is engaged — "بتخططوا لعرس؟ / מתכננים חתונה?" is the register, not "we saw you're getting married."
- **Ground every claim in a real feature.** Proof-of-delivery photos, per-guest WhatsApp links, RSVP dashboard, digital invitation designs — these are true. Don't promise capabilities Dawa doesn't have.
