# Competitor Landscape

A first competitor scan for [[Dawa]], created 2026-06-20 as part of the
[[CRO and IA Audit 2026-06-20]]. The owner reported being "somewhat blind" on
competitors, so this is the starting map — **validate the local/informal column
against what your last ~10 real customers actually compared you to.**

## The one-line takeaway

Dawa's **digital invitation** is a commodity — there are free and near-free tools
that make a bilingual RTL digital invite with WhatsApp RSVP. Dawa's **moat is the
operation around it**: physical hand-delivery, per-address photo proof, live driver
GPS, and the AWS-Rekognition photo-finder ([[Face Matching]]). **Sell the
operation, not the invite.** Pricing the digital plan as "a nicer digital invite"
invites a "but Canva is free" objection; pricing it as "we run your whole
invitation logistics end-to-end, done-for-you, in your dialect" does not.

## The field (3 buckets)

### 1. DIY / free-or-cheap digital (the pricing-pressure threat)
- **QuikRSVP** — multilingual incl. Hebrew RTL + Arabic; free tier (3 forms, 25
  responses), AI translation, QR, WhatsApp share, analytics.
- **Pippit** — digital Arabic invitation creator; WhatsApp/email share + RSVP.
- **Canva / Etsy / Zazzle templates** — self-serve template + manual WhatsApp send.
- **withJoy** — free wedding site + RSVP + matching prints (global, English-first).
> These set the *anchor price* in a couple's head at ≈ ₪0. The digital plan must
> clear that anchor on **service + outcome**, never on "it's a digital invite."

### 2. Culturally-aligned digital (closest positioning overlap)
- **Mahabba Wedding** — digital wedding invitations *for Muslims* + RSVP
  management. Closest to Dawa's audience framing on the digital side — but still
  digital-only, no delivery/logistics layer.
- **Save This Date (save-date.com)** — RSVP site + WhatsApp/SMS invitations
  (Israel). Overlaps with Dawa's WhatsApp-RSVP flow ([[WhatsApp Messaging]]).

### 3. Traditional print + manual delivery (what Dawa actually replaces)
- **Local Arab-society print shops** — design + print physical invitations; the
  couple (or family) hand-delivers themselves or via relatives. **This is the real
  incumbent** for the handwritten/physical plan — informal, relationship-based,
  hard to find online. Validate names with customers.
- Jewish-market analogues (Cohen Printing, Total RSVP) show the same model
  elsewhere: print + addressing/RSVP services, no GPS-tracked delivery proof.

## What NOBODY in the field does (Dawa's defensible differentiators)
1. **Physical hand-delivery as a managed service** with uniformed drivers.
2. **Per-address photo proof + timestamp + recipient name** ([[Digital Invitations]] / driver flow).
3. **Live driver GPS** the groom watches from their phone ([[Polling and Realtime]]).
4. **Face-recognition photo-finder** so guests retrieve their event photos ([[Face Matching]]).
5. **Dialect-aware, done-for-you operation** — the admin sends every message; the couple does nothing.

## Objection table (feed this into the WhatsApp sales script)
| Prospect says | Honest reframe |
|---|---|
| "I can make a digital invite free on Canva." | "True — and you'll still spend a week chasing 400 RSVPs by hand. We run the whole thing: send, track, and you watch confirmations live." |
| "Why pay for delivery? My cousin can drop them off." | "Will your cousin photograph every delivery with the time and who received it? We give you proof of every single one." |
| "₪2,500 is a lot for a digital invite." | Don't defend the invite — anchor on the operation + the photo-finder + zero effort. Quote the physical plan's delivery proof as the headline. |
| "How do I know it'll look good?" | Send the live sample link (`/d/demo/demo?demo=1`) — surfaced on the landing page as "see a live sample." |
