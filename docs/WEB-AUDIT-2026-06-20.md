# PART I — Lead-Consultant Decision Document

## 1. Executive summary

Dawa is a **deeply built, well-engineered product whose biggest problems are config and operations, not code.** Across all five domains the recurring pattern is the same: the hard engineering is done and shipped (RTL i18n at parity, bespoke design system, 3-layer security with signature-verified webhooks, biometric face-purge, admin analytics, CI suite, backup scripts), but the value is *gated behind unset switches and unwatched operations*. Two of the brief's scariest assumptions turned out to be false on inspection (see §3).

The 3–5 things that matter most:

1. **The funnel is wired but dead at the top.** `/api/settings/public` returns `{}` → no WhatsApp business number → every "book via WhatsApp" CTA silently falls back to the *login wall* (the worst outcome for a prospect). One ~2-minute admin-config field unlocks the entire coded funnel. Flagged independently by Strategy, Copy, Technical, and Design. **This is the single highest value-to-effort action on the site.**
2. **There is no financial guardrail.** No GCP budget alert, no AWS budget alert, no `maxInstances` cap on the `api` function. For a no-budget operator, a polling bug or a runaway Rekognition re-index is the difference between a ₪5 and a ₪500 surprise. Free, ~15 minutes — and a prerequisite before turning on any paid integration.
3. **The survival baseline (backups + monitoring) is coded but switched off.** `backupRtdb` is a no-op until `BACKUP_BUCKET` is set; `setup-backups.sh` is unrun; restore is untested; `/api/health` exists but nothing watches it. All fixable on free tiers in an afternoon, and existential for a solo operator with PII + biometric data in three datastores.
4. **A written privacy promise the system may not keep.** The Terms promise 30-day deletion. The *biometric* purge is genuinely shipped (verified) — but *general* guest PII (names/phones/addresses) is not auto-deleted. The honest, cheap fix is to align the policy wording with reality (needs your sign-off); building a purge job is optional.
5. **Two brief assumptions were wrong — in your favour.** The "frontend build is broken" claim is **false** (build verified healthy). The "admin-SDK key is leaked in git history and blocks co-maintainer handoff" claim is **false** (key is gitignored, never committed, on-disk only). The handoff is far less blocked than the docs say; the real action is to *correct the docs* and move the on-disk key out of OneDrive — not a destructive history rewrite.

The product doesn't need more features right now. It needs its existing machinery **switched on, watched, and protected by a budget cap.**

## Strategy / Business audit — Dawa (دعوة)

Lens: solo owner-operator, no deadline, no cash budget. Every verdict favours cheap/free, low-maintenance, high-leverage. The standout fact this domain keeps colliding with: **a deep, defensible product is being throttled by two near-zero-effort gaps — an unset WhatsApp number that makes the whole funnel inert, and zero top-of-funnel measurement.** Almost everything else is "later" because the binding constraint isn't strategy artifacts, it's that the front door is locked and the operator can only run ~20–30 weddings by hand.

### 1. Positioning & moat (delivery + proof + photo-finder vs commodity invite)
- **Need:** Critical. In a market where a bilingual RTL digital invite is free (Canva, QuikRSVP), positioning *is* the business. If the pitch is "a nicer digital invite," the anchor price is ₪0 and ₪2,500 looks absurd.
- **Current state:** Already articulated, and well. `wiki/Competitor-Landscape.md` nails it: "Dawa's digital invitation is a commodity... the moat is the operation around it — physical hand-delivery, per-address photo proof, live driver GPS, AWS-Rekognition photo-finder. Sell the operation, not the invite." Five defensible differentiators are enumerated. The growth-loop credit even reflects it ("صُنعت بواسطة دعوة" in `DigitalInvitationView.jsx:247`).
- **Value impact:** High. The thinking is done and correct; the gap is that it lives in a wiki, not in the customer-facing surface or sales script.
- **Verdict:** **now** (cheap) — the *analysis* is already-done; the *deployment of the message* is the work, and it's free.
- **Implementation:** (1) Make the landing hero + pricing copy (`frontend/src/i18n/ar.js` lines ~79–125) lead with the operation (uniformed delivery + photo proof + photo-finder), not the invite aesthetic. (2) Paste the `Competitor-Landscape.md` objection table verbatim into a saved WhatsApp quick-reply set. Zero new infra.
- **Edge cases:** Over-indexing on the physical operation alienates the "modern/digital" segment who want the digital plan + photo-finder.
- **Handling:** Two message tracks (the persona page already flags traditional vs modern) — physical-dignity track and digital+photo-finder track; pick based on the prospect's first message.
- **Gaps:** The moat is operationally real but **undocumented as proof on the site** — no public sample of a proof photo / GPS screenshot / photo-finder demo beyond `/d/demo/demo?demo=1`. Social proof (named testimonials, a real delivery-proof screenshot) is the single highest-leverage missing asset in a reputation-driven market.

### 2. Pricing model & packaging (hardcoded amounts; physical not self-serve)
- **Need:** Yes — pricing is the conversion lever and it's currently brittle and unvalidated.
- **Current state:** Digital Premium ₪2,500 / VIP ₪3,500 hardcoded in **two** places: backend `PLAN_AMOUNTS_ILS` (`backend/functions/src/api/routes/payments.ts:33`) AND frontend display copy (`frontend/src/i18n/ar.js:105,115` + `:325,326`). Physical plan is sold via WhatsApp conversation, no self-serve price at all. The code itself flags the dual-source problem: payments.ts comments "Later: move to adminSettings so the operator can change prices without a deploy." Stripe is wired (test mode) — Payment Links created on the fly, admin-driven. *(Verifier: it's actually 5 sources — see callout.)*
- **Value impact:** Medium. The dual hardcode is a small maintenance footgun (change one, forget the other → backend charges ₪2,500 while the page says something else). The bigger issue is the **price hasn't been validated against the free-tool anchor** (`Competitor-Landscape.md` open follow-up explicitly asks whether the digital price holds or needs bundling).
- **Verdict:** **next** — don't build the "move prices to adminSettings" feature yet (premature for a solo op who deploys anyway); instead (a) reconcile the hardcoded values so they can't drift, and (b) make a *pricing decision*: keep digital priced as "logistics operation + photo-finder," not "a digital invite."
- **Implementation:** Cheap path — leave amounts hardcoded but add a one-line comment/test asserting i18n display matches `PLAN_AMOUNTS_ILS` so drift is caught. Defer the adminSettings migration until the operator actually wants to A/B a price.
- **Edge cases:** Moving prices to a DB-editable setting adds an admin surface + a place to fat-finger a ₪25 charge; for one operator who controls deploys, it's negative ROI right now.
- **Handling:** Skip the migration; a single source-of-truth reconciliation (or a unit test) gets 80% of the safety for 5% of the effort.
- **Gaps:** No **physical-plan price** anywhere in code — it's entirely tribal knowledge in the operator's head. No bundle (physical + photo-finder) SKU even though the moat narrative implies one. No deposit/partial-payment option (weddings are high-ticket; a deposit lowers the commit barrier).

### 3. Conversion funnel incl. the inert WhatsApp-number blocker
- **Need:** Critical — this is THE binding constraint.
- **Current state:** Funnel is fully wired but **dead at the top**. Verified live this session: `GET https://dawa-aa793.web.app/api/settings/public` → `{}` (HTTP 200). `LandingPage.jsx:238-241` shows every "book" CTA degrades to the portal login wall when no number is set ("the whole WhatsApp-primary funnel depends on that number"). So a high-intent prospect who clicks "book" hits a *login screen* — the worst possible outcome.
- **Value impact:** High. This is a ~2-minute config change (admin Communication Settings → set WhatsApp business number) standing between a coded funnel and a working one. Highest value-to-effort item in the entire domain.
- **Verdict:** **now** — trivial, free, unblocks everything downstream.
- **Implementation:** Admin portal → Communication Settings tab → enter WhatsApp business number in international digits (the i18n hint at `ar.js:303` shows the format `972500000000`) → enable. Re-verify `/api/settings/public` returns the number and the landing CTA opens WhatsApp.
- **Edge cases:** Wrong/typo'd number silently routes leads to a dead chat; the number must be a WhatsApp-registered line; if the operator uses a personal number, privacy/burnout.
- **Handling:** After setting, click the live CTA once and confirm the chat opens to the right inbox. Consider a dedicated WhatsApp Business number to separate work/personal.
- **Gaps:** Even once set, **the click is unmeasured** (see item 6) — you'll have a working funnel you can't read. Set the number first, instrument second.

### 4. High-touch vs self-serve signup & the ~20–30 operator ceiling
- **Need:** Real but **not yet binding**. At current volume the manual model is fine; the ceiling only bites near 20–30 concurrent weddings.
- **Current state:** Signup is admin-provisioned grooms only — no self-serve. Every guest WhatsApp send is operator-driven (the moat narrative leans on "the admin sends every message; the couple does nothing"). That's a feature for positioning and a ceiling for scale simultaneously.
- **Value impact:** Medium now, High later. High-touch is currently a *quality + moat* asset, not a bug. Self-serve signup would erode the curated, dignity-first positioning.
- **Verdict:** **later** — do not build self-serve signup; it's expensive, dilutes positioning, and solves a problem you don't have yet. Instead, when the ceiling nears, attack the *send* toil, not the signup.
- **Implementation:** When volume warrants, the leverage is in (a) WhatsApp template-batch send (already coded — `photoShare.routes.ts` does bulk-send; the same pattern can serve invite-send) and (b) hiring/delegating the operator role, not removing it. The infra to send in bulk largely exists; it's gated on WhatsApp creds being live.
- **Edge cases:** Automating sends risks WhatsApp rate-limits / template rejection / spam flags that could ban the business number — catastrophic for a one-number operation.
- **Handling:** Keep sends human-paced and template-approved until volume forces batching; treat the WhatsApp number as a critical, hard-to-replace asset.
- **Gaps:** No documented "second operator" onboarding runbook — the ceiling is partly a *handoff* problem. Bus-factor = 1.

### 5. Growth loop ("Made with Dawa") + its measurement
- **Need:** High — in a word-of-mouth, reputation-driven market, the invite seen by 100–600 in-market guests per wedding is the cheapest, most native acquisition channel that exists.
- **Current state:** The loop is **shipped**: `DigitalInvitationView.jsx:243-249` renders a public-only credit linking `/?ref=invite`, and `LandingPage.jsx:220-228` folds the `ref` into the WhatsApp booking message so the operator sees the source. But it has **no click-tracking** — you can't tell if it ever produced a lead except by eyeballing the "(وصلت عبر: invite)" suffix in chats.
- **Value impact:** High leverage, currently unmeasurable. Each digital wedding broadcasts the loop to hundreds of exactly-right prospects for free.
- **Verdict:** **next** — the loop exists (don't rebuild it); the cheap win is making its output *legible*. The operator-visible `ref` suffix is already a poor-man's tracker.
- **Implementation:** Free path: formalize the existing suffix as the measurement — operator tags each lead's source. Better path (ties to item 6): a no-PII counter endpoint that increments on `?ref=invite` landing hits. The Conversion-KPIs page flags this needs a DB-writing endpoint and defers it per the "ask before DB changes" rule.
- **Edge cases:** A counter endpoint is an unauthenticated write → abuse/inflation risk; bots hitting `/?ref=invite` pollute the number.
- **Handling:** Rate-limit + dedupe by session; or skip the endpoint and rely on the operator-visible `ref` suffix (already shipped). For a solo op, the suffix may be enough.
- **Gaps:** The credit is **digital-invite-only** — the physical invitation (the actual moat product) carries no loop. A printed QR or small "made by Dawa" line on the physical card would seed the loop in the segment that values physical most. No referral incentive to amplify the loop.

### 6. Business-health measurement (top-of-funnel clicks, invite-opens)
- **Need:** High — "you can't improve the funnel step you can't see," and the two top steps (WhatsApp CTA click, invite-open) are both dark.
- **Current state:** Mid-funnel and down is instrumented: the Admin Analytics command center (live 2026-06-19) covers RSVP %, delivery-proof %, ARPU/revenue from Stripe. The North Star (paid weddings/month) and KPI framework are defined. But **WhatsApp leads/week is "not measured"** and **invite-open is untracked** — so `lead → paid %` is not computable. The KPI page is explicit: "a speedometer with no destination," no baselines, no targets.
- **Value impact:** High. Without the top-of-funnel count, every other metric floats — you can measure that you converted, never your conversion *rate*.
- **Verdict:** **now (the free half) / next (the endpoint half).** The free win is non-code: set targets after a 2-week baseline, run the 15-min weekly review the KPI page prescribes. The CTA-click counter is a small build, gated on a DB-write decision.
- **Implementation:** (1) Free now: once the WhatsApp number is live (item 3), record leads/week manually from incoming chats (the `ref` suffix helps) → that IS the baseline. (2) Then: lightweight `?ref`/CTA counter endpoint (no PII), surfaced in Admin Analytics. Invite-open tracking is heavier (pixel/beacon) and lower priority.
- **Edge cases:** Unauthenticated counters invite inflation; invite-open beacons raise privacy questions in a privacy-flagged product.
- **Handling:** Start with manual lead counting (zero code, zero privacy surface). Only build the endpoint once manual counting proves the loop produces leads worth optimizing.
- **Gaps:** The real gap is discipline, not tooling — the framework exists; the operator must run the weekly review.

### 7. Buyer-persona validation
